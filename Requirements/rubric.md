# Executive Summary
Quality and completeness of the executive summary including problem statement, why this work is important/valuable, the structure of the document, key results and findings

5 to >4.0 pts
Exemplary
Required details are included in the executive summary. The summary is concise and the key project problems, results, and findings are highlighted.

# Project background and problem statement
What is the problem to be solved?
Why is it important to address this problem?
Who will use it and what value will they get out of it

5 to >4.0 pts
Exemplary
Details about the organization, context, and the problem to be solved are clearly presented with appropriate references. The motivations and contributions of the project are clearly presented and supported by sound arguments and appropriate evidences. Ultimately, this section convinces the readers that the project is worth doing and able to produce results that are promising and meaningful.

# Literature Review and Market research
What gaps have other people discovered that you could help fill?
Market research and literature review identifies the gaps and shows a very clear direction to seek a solution that is innovative and based on solid research. A comprehensive set of innovative design/research questions are identified that will provide a clear guide for an innovative project.

10 to >8.0 pts
Exemplary
The reviews are comprehensive and insightful, and written in an appropriate format for professional reports. Critical discussions and synthesis are provided. The readers can clearly see how the project fits into the relevant field and industry.
