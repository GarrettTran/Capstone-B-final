Assessment Details
In this assignment you need to provide the following information

Executive summary
It is a summary of your project, providing information about the context/background, the problem that needs to be solved, the solution that will be developed at the end of the project, and its significance/contributions to practice and theory.
Recommended length: 1 page
Project background and problem statement
In this section, you will provide the background of the project that links to the problems to be solved. Furthermore, you will provide the motivation and significance of the project in practice i.e., how it would be used.
The project sponsor (i.e., industry partner or faculty member) will be able to provide relevant advice and information here to shape this section, however it is your arguments and research will bring out the significance of the project. That is, the project is significant not because "my supervisor says so" or because "it will be used by the company", but you need to think about how the outcomes of the project will be used in the wider and generalized context. For instance, your machine learning solution will help to improve the current accuracy of predicting the company's employee turnover from 70% to 90% (context-specific), and how does this solution be used in human resource management in general?
Recommended length: 2 pages
Literature review and market research
In this section, you will perform market research and literature review to determine (a) the current and alternative products to what you are working on and (b) the techniques/designs/technologies that are relevant to your solution. For example, if your project includes designing a visualization dashboard, then you may compare it with existing products such as PowerBI, Tableau, Qlik etc. On the other hand, if you are working on a deep learning problem, then your literature review may include the different neural network architects that you may consider for your project.
From doing these market research and literature review, you will be able to justify your project's contributions more clearly and critically. It will also help you to make plans for your project. Besides the practical usefulness of your project, you may want to discuss how your project can contribute to and expand theoretical knowledge as well if applicable.
Update from progress report & completion plan: provide additional market and literature review for any changes or new considerations that you may have for your project. For instance, if your project has expanded its scope and a new function is needed, then you will provide a literature review on the technologies that are relevant to that new function or scope.
Recommended length: 4 to 6 pages
Solution design
In this section, you will first remind the readers about the scope of work and the performance metrics that were discussed in the previous reports (i.e. project proposal, progress report, completion plan), including any changes that happened during your journey so far.
Also in this section, you will provide a detailed design of the project solution. This design will include descriptions of the solution's functions, users, the technologies that have been considered by the team, rationale behind your architecture and design, and required resources.
Depending on your specific discipline, you will provide the relevant technical diagrams and visualizations that you have studied in previous courses, in order to present the project specifications by following established professional standards. If you have tables and complex diagrams that provide detailed specifications and designs, you should consider putting them in the Appendix and referring to these tables and diagrams in the main body of the report.
Recommended length: 6 to 10 pages
Result analyses & discussions 
Present key results and accomplishments of your capstone projects. Do you accomplish all key deliverables proposed in the project proposal and meet or exceed all projects requirements?
Analyze and discuss the results and findings, performance and key characteristics of the final product, potential issues for future work and consideration.
Recommended length: 4 to 6 pages
Summary & reflection
In this section, you will reflect on the capstone project journey so far to identify what worked and what did not work, and discuss the reasons and lessons learned that you have drawn from working on the project. At the end of your reflection, you will discuss the key considerations to ensure success in the next phases of your project and provide some recommendations.
Please note that the reflection should be objective and constructive, and written from the perspective of the whole team as a whole rather than reflecting individual's viewpoints. This section should also not be used to name and criticize individuals for their mistakes, or to blame people or situations for undesirable events that took place in your journey. You are encouraged to discuss as a team to decide the key points to be included in this section first before starting to write it.
Recommended length: 1 page
